# Commodity Trading & Credit Risk Analytics

> JPMorgan Chase & Co. Quantitative Research Virtual Experience (Forage)

A collection of quantitative finance models developed as part of the **JPMorgan Chase Quantitative Research Virtual Experience Program** on Forage. The project focuses on commodity price forecasting, storage contract valuation, and credit risk modeling using Python and statistical learning techniques.

---

## Overview

This project consists of four tasks simulating the work of a Quantitative Research Analyst at JPMorgan Chase.

The work covers:

- Commodity price forecasting
- Natural gas storage valuation
- Credit risk modeling
- Probability of Default (PD) estimation

---

## Task 1: Commodity Price Forecasting

Developed a statistical forecasting model for natural gas prices by combining:

- Linear Regression
- Trend Estimation
- Seasonal (Sinusoidal) Modelling
- Time-series Interpolation & Extrapolation

### Techniques

- Python
- NumPy
- Pandas
- Matplotlib

---

## Task 2: Natural Gas Storage Valuation

Implemented a valuation framework for natural gas storage contracts considering:

- Injection schedules
- Withdrawal schedules
- Storage costs
- Transaction costs
- Cash flow optimization

### Concepts

- Commodity Trading
- Storage Optimization
- Financial Modelling

---

## Task 3: Credit Risk Modelling

Developed a credit risk prediction model using:

- Logistic Regression
- Credit Feature Engineering
- FICO Score Analysis
- Probability of Default Prediction

### Libraries

- Scikit-Learn
- Pandas
- NumPy

---

## Task 4: Probability of Default Estimation

Implemented Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) for:

- FICO score bucketing
- Credit risk segmentation
- Probability of Default estimation

### Concepts

- Maximum Likelihood Estimation
- Dynamic Programming
- Credit Risk Analytics
